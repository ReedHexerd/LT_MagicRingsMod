# LT_MagicRingsMod
Mod Name: **MAGIC☆RINGS** 
Mod Category: **Encounter/Items/Combat Moves/Status Effects**

Description:
Adds a ton of stuff where you go on a quest to get yourself magic rings! (Or just skip it if you don't wanna have the hassle. ~~*I'll be silently judging you tho*~~)
- Said encounter has a **15%** chance to trigger daily in the **Dominion Alleyways**.
- Given by someone from said encounter. (Don't worry, I left hints on how to find them.)
- Some rings can be spawned in the alleyways as well, so keep an eye out! 
- For people with the old mod, just download over it! There's an event that I've added just for you guys~ 

**ver 0.3:** Added EVEN MORE STUFF! 
- **Simpler transformation!** Now you just need to use the new item rings in order to transform anywhere! 
- **Added a NEW NPC!** She wanders around the **Dominion alleyways** during 4am to 9pm, then in the Watering Hole at 9pm to 4am. She *might* mysteriously disappear around 12, but maybe you can find her if you search around a bit... 
- You can now buy stuff from the ringmaker! What stuff? Anything made by yours truly~ 
- Expanded on the ringmaker's lore!
- **HARDER BOSS BATTLE!** Beware of guns!
- Added some more events centered around the ringmaker (one in the alleyways, one in the shop), resets daily! 

**ver 0.2:** Added more stuff! 
- **UPDATED MAGICAL GIRL CLOTHING!** Thanks to @solace for their incredible work! 
- Now during the chance encounter, you can choose between the normal bracelet and a femboy version (for any fans with that particular kink)! Don't worry, ver0.1-ers, you can just go to the local ringmaker and she'll give it to you~ Thanks @rukume for the idea! 
- You can get a different magical chant if you wear the Dark Siren outfit, and with the scythe in your hand. Make sure to look at your combat moves~
- Added managers for sex scenes, and said ringmaker can finally get pregnant.

How to Install: Unpack the zip file and put it into the `res/mods` folder.
